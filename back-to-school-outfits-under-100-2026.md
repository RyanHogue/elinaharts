## Why "6 situations" instead of a top-10 list

September is the single biggest month for back-to-school shopping three years running, and
three-quarters of families spend at least $100 on clothing alone. But most back-to-school roundups
just rank hoodies by price. That doesn't answer the actual question a parent or student has:
*"what do I wear on the first day, what do I bring for the classroom that's always freezing, what
works for gym day."*

This page answers that instead — six real products, checked in stock on **August 3, 2026**, each
matched to one specific situation.
