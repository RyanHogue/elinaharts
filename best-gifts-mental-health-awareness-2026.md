## The 988 Lifeline: What It Actually Is

Call or text **988** anytime (24/7) from anywhere in the U.S. to reach a trained crisis counselor. It's free, confidential, and doesn't cost the person receiving the call. No call to 911 required.

**Who can call?**
- People in immediate crisis
- People thinking about suicide
- People struggling with depression, anxiety, trauma, grief, addiction

**What happens?**
- You talk to a real human counselor within minutes
- They listen without judgment
- They help you stay safe and find next steps (therapy, emergency services, community resources)

Wearing a 988 hoodie means you care. Knowing the number matters too.
