## How we chose

We pulled Stay Another Day's live product feed on **July 28, 2026** and filtered it down to every
listing with "Hoodie" or "Hooded Sweatshirt" in the title — **34 listings total**. From those we
dropped 2 youth-sized hoodies (different sizing entirely) and 1 leftover single-variant listing
("Vibrant – SAD Crewneck Hoodie," no size options, effectively discontinued stock), leaving **31
adult hoodie designs** to choose from.

For each one we recorded price, color count, the full size run, and how many of its variants were
actually marked in stock at the moment we checked — not just "sizes offered," which can include
sizes that are sold out. We set aside the 988 design and the embroidered lineup, since those already
have dedicated guides (linked above), and picked one design per remaining situation (tightest
budget, no-graphic, boldest design, Pride Month, Valentine's, and the one to be careful with) rather
than ranking all 31 top-to-bottom, since most of them share the same $55 price and S–5XL size run —
the real differences are the graphic, the color options, and whether it's actually in stock.

**What we can't judge:** how the screen print or embroidery holds up after washes, or how the
fleece feels on skin. We don't have these garments in hand — see the limitations in our
[full brand review]({{ '/stay-another-day-review-2026' | relative_url }}).

## The picks

### 💰 Cheapest full-size-range hoodie — SAD x Royalty Hoops Hoodie ($40)

At **$40**, this is the least expensive hoodie in the catalog that still runs the full size range
— here labeled **Small through 5X** rather than 5XL, same ceiling. It's a collaboration design (SAD
x Royalty Hoops) in **8 colors**, including Sport Grey, Army Green and Purple — a wider color set
than most of the $55+ designs. All **62 of 62 variants** were in stock at the time we checked. Being
a collab piece, treat it as a drop that may not restock once it sells out, rather than a permanent
catalog staple.

### 🎨 No big graphic, just a solid color — Better Together Hooded Sweatshirt ($54.95)

Every other hoodie on this page has a printed or embroidered design on the front. This one doesn't
— it's a **plain, solid-color hooded sweatshirt** in **Beige, Black, Brown, or White**, sized
**Small through 5XL**, with **8 of 8 variants in stock** in each color. It still carries the "Better
Together – Better Days Are Coming" name tied to the brand's mission, just without a loud graphic —
the pick if you want to support the cause without wearing a visible message.

### 🌈 Brightest / most colorful design — Layered Rainbow Screen Printed Hoodie ($55)

Most of the catalog's designs are single-color prints on neutral hoodie colors. This one is the
outlier — a layered rainbow graphic, available across **6 hoodie colors** (Black, Beige, Brown,
Gray, Light Blue, Light Pink), **S–5XL**, **48 of 48 variants in stock**. If the muted, minimalist
look of the rest of the catalog isn't what you're after, this is the one design that stands apart
from it.

### 🏳️‍🌈 Pride Month specifically — Pride Month 2026 Exclusive Hoodie ($55)

A dedicated **Pride Month 2026** design, currently offered in **Black only**, sizes **S–5XL**, all
**8 of 8 variants in stock**. Being labeled an "exclusive," it's reasonable to expect this one won't
carry over indefinitely — if the specific 2026 design matters to you, buy it while it's listed
rather than assuming it'll still be there later in the year.

### 💝 Valentine's / romantic gift — Valentine's Day Printed Hoodie, Pink Heart ($55)

A small heart graphic rather than a hood-to-hood message design — a lighter option if you want
something festive without wearing the brand's cause messaging front and center. Offered in **5
colors** (Black, Beige, Brown, Light Blue, Light Pink), **S–5XL**, **40 of 40 variants** in stock. A
matching **Red Heart** version exists too, in Black and Light Pink only, also S–5XL and fully in
stock. Both are seasonal listings — buy while they're live rather than assuming they carry into
next year unchanged.

### ⚠️ Buy with caution — Camo Premium Hoodie ($75)

This is the most expensive hoodie in the catalog, and it's also the one with the weakest
availability: only **3 of 7 variants (43%) were in stock** at the time we checked. It's also one of
only **two hoodie designs in the entire 31-design lineup that don't reach 5XL** — sizing tops out at
**3XL** here (the other is the limited "March Exclusive – 99 Reasons to Stay Another Day Hoodie,"
also 3XL). If extended sizing matters to you, the camo print isn't the design to build that
expectation around — nearly every other $55 screen-printed hoodie in the catalog runs the full
S–5XL range instead.

## The one sizing exception worth knowing

Our [sizing guide]({{ '/stay-another-day-sizing-guide-2026' | relative_url }}) found that hoodies
reach 5XL on the large majority of styles, versus tees and long sleeves which mostly cap at 3XL.
Looking at hoodies specifically, that pattern holds even more strongly than it looked at the
brand-wide level: **28 of the 30 sized adult hoodie designs (93%) run all the way to 5XL.** The
only two that don't — the **Camo Premium Hoodie** and the **March Exclusive "99 Reasons" Hoodie** —
both stop at 3XL. Neither is flagged with any fit warning on the product page; the smaller range is
simply how those two specific listings are built. If you wear above 3XL, it's worth confirming the
size run on the exact product page before buying either of those two, even though every other
hoodie in the catalog will carry your size.
