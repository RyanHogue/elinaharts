## What Consciously Baby actually is

Despite the name, this is **not** a K-12 back-to-school clothing brand. The real catalog splits into two very different product lines:

1. **Soft-sole leather boots, sandals, and Mary Janes for infants and toddlers** (sizes labeled by age, e.g., "3–6 months," "6–9 months") — footwear, not clothing, and for babies, not school-age kids.
2. **Graphic tees for adult women**, sized XS–2X, with slogan prints (some edgy/adult phrasing, not classroom-appropriate).

There is no published item in the catalog for school-age children's polos or basic tees as the original version of this page claimed.

---

## The two real product lines

### Baby & toddler footwear ($26–$60)
- Waxed Leather Chelsea Boots, Combat Boots, Mary Janes, woven sandals
- Sizes run by age band (infant through toddler), soft-sole and hard-sole versions
- Example: Waxed Leather Chelsea Boot, $25.99, sizes "2 (3–6 months)" through toddler sizes

### Women's graphic tees ($15–$36)
- Relaxed-fit graphic tees, sizes XS–2X/3X depending on design
- Slogan-driven prints — check wording before buying if you want something workplace- or classroom-neutral, as some designs use casual profanity
