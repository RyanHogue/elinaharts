CRZ Yoga's storefront is huge — close to **1,200 active styles** at the time of writing — and
almost none of it is sorted by fabric the way a shopper actually thinks about it. Instead, the
brand splits its leggings, shorts and tops into **named collections** (Butterluxe, Butterlift,
Nakedfeel, Feathery-Fit, Pima Cotton, Secretsculpt...), and the name is really a stand-in for a
different fabric blend and a different level of compression.

If you've landed on the site wondering *"what's actually the difference between Butterluxe and
Butterlift"* — that's the right question, and this guide answers it using the brand's own listed
fabric percentages, not marketing copy.

## The six fabric collections, decoded

We read the product description of a representative item in each named collection and pulled the
**actual listed fabric percentage** — not the marketing adjectives around it.

| Collection | Fabric composition | Feel, per the brand | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Butterluxe** | 81% Polyamide, 19% Lycra | Extremely soft, lightweight, gentle compression | Yoga, lounging, all-day wear |
| **Butterlift** | 74% Polyamide, 26% Lycra | Soft but noticeably more compression than Butterluxe | Workout, squats, more support |
| **Nakedfeel** | Buttery-soft, slick, 4-way stretch (brand does not publish exact %) | Decent compression, smooth "no-fur-stick" feel | Hot yoga, Pilates |
| **Feathery-Fit** | 86% Polyester, 14% Elastane | Weightless, quick-drying, no swish sound | Running, cardio |
| **Pima Cotton** | 92% Pima cotton, 8% Spandex | Soft, breathable, natural fiber | Casual wear, low-impact days |
| **Secretsculpt** | Same fabric as Butterlift (74% Polyamide, 26% Lycra) | Seamless construction, no front seam | Leggings worn as the main visible layer |

**The practical takeaway:** if a listing just says "buttery soft" without a number, that's not a red
flag by itself — Nakedfeel is one of the brand's most-reviewed lines and simply doesn't publish an
exact ratio on every page. But when you're choosing between two collections that both claim "soft,"
the fabric percentage is the tie-breaker: **higher Lycra/Elastane % = more compression and structure,
higher Polyamide/Polyester % = softer, lighter drape.**

Butterluxe is by far the largest collection — **381 of the roughly 1,189 total styles**, spanning
leggings, tanks, jackets and even a maternity sub-line. Feathery-Fit (109 styles) and Pima Cotton
(63 styles) are meaningfully smaller. Secretsculpt is the newest and smallest at just 13 styles.

## Size range — the part to check before you buy

This is the biggest limitation we found. Every collection runs a standard **XXS–XL** (occasionally
XS–XL) range as the default, and **XXL is the exception, not the rule**:

| Collection | Styles offering XXL |
|---|---|
| Feathery-Fit | 21 of 109 (~19%) |
| Butterluxe | 19 of 381 (~5%) |
| Pima Cotton | 4 of 63 (~6%) |
| Nakedfeel | 3 of 70 (~4%) |
| Butterlift | 2 of 41 (~5%) |
| Secretsculpt | 0 of 13 |

Beyond XXL, sizing is essentially absent — the catalog-wide count of 1X–4X listings is **one product
each**, not a real plus-size range. **If you wear above a standard XL, check the specific product
page's size options before adding to cart — don't assume the collection you want offers your size.**

There's also a dedicated **maternity line** (51 styles across the Butterluxe Maternity and other
sub-lines) and a smaller **men's range** (104 styles) and **girls' range** (51 styles), but the
women's non-maternity catalog is where the vast majority of the depth is.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between Butterluxe and Butterlift?**
Both are soft polyamide/Lycra blends, but Butterlift has a higher Lycra ratio (26% vs. 19%) and is
built for more compression and support — better for workouts, while Butterluxe leans softer and is
positioned for yoga and lounging.

**Does CRZ Yoga run true to size?**
The brand does not publish a "runs small/runs large" statement across its catalog in the product
data we reviewed. Standard sizing is XXS–XL across most collections; treat the specific size chart
on each product page as the source of truth rather than assuming consistency across collections.

**Does CRZ Yoga have plus sizes?**
Coverage is thin. Most collections offer XXL on only a small minority of styles (roughly 4–19%
depending on the collection), and sizes above XXL are essentially one-off listings, not a real range.

**What is Secretsculpt?**
It's CRZ Yoga's newest, smallest collection (13 styles) — leggings made from the same fabric as
Butterlift, but constructed with no front seam for a smoother look under fitted clothing.

**Is CRZ Yoga good for hot yoga?**
The brand markets its Nakedfeel collection specifically for hot yoga and Pilates, describing it as
sweat-wicking with a "no-fur-stick" feel and decent compression — that's the collection to start
with if that's your main use case.

**How many products does CRZ Yoga sell?**
Close to **1,189 active styles** across women's, maternity, men's and girls' lines at the time of
writing, spanning six named fabric collections plus a number of smaller one-off lines.
