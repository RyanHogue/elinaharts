## How we chose

We pulled live product data from all four brands we track on **July 28, 2026** and picked one hoodie
per common "type of dad," rather than one ranked list — the right pick depends on his size, his
climate, and what he actually likes, not a single winner. Each pick had to be **in stock at the time
we checked** and have a **verifiable spec** (published fabric weight, size range, or price point) we
could point to instead of a vague claim.

We deliberately skipped anything with a political or divisive slogan while browsing Farm Brand's
catalog (which carries several message-driven designs) — a Father's Day gift guide isn't the place
for that, regardless of the message.

**What we can't judge:** how any of these fit a specific body, or how they hold up after a year of
wear. We don't have these garments in hand — see each brand's full review for what we can and can't
assess without the product in hand.

## The picks

### 🥶 Feels the cold the most — Resilience Heavyweight Cotton Hoodie, Jamroc Dept ($165)

At **530 GSM**, this is the heaviest hoodie fabric across all four brands we track — genuinely
blanket-weight cotton, not marketing language. Available in Black, Dark Gray, Coffee, and Retro
Green, all **24 of 24 variants in stock**. The one limitation: this specific design runs **2XS
through XL only** — no plus sizing on this particular hoodie, unlike most of Jamroc's catalog. If he
wears above XL, use the next pick instead.

### 📏 Wears above XL — Island Motion Oversized Hoodie, Jamroc Dept ($155)

Runs **2XS all the way to 6XL** — one of the widest size ranges in streetwear, on **11 of 11
variants in stock**. It's an oversized, all-over print design (yellow-based abstract print with
green and black brushwork) rather than a plain hoodie, so it suits a dad who already leans toward
bold streetwear rather than basics. Jamroc doesn't publish a GSM figure for this specific design.

### 🏄 Is at the beach every chance he gets — Ripper Hoodie Brown, Surf Trip Supply ($98)

Surf Trip Supply is a California surf-culture brand start to finish, and this is one of its
best-stocked designs — **4 of 4 variants in stock**, sizes S–XL, listed as **100% cotton** (the
brand doesn't publish a GSM figure for it). If he already owns Surf Trip Supply pieces or spends his
weekends near water, this reads as a brand he'd pick for himself, not a generic gift hoodie.

### 💰 Buying on a budget — Just Right Surf Hoodie, Farm Brand ($40)

The best-documented budget hoodie we track: **281 GSM** (8.3 oz), 80/20 ring-spun cotton/polyester,
sizes **XS through 3XL**, **35 of 35 variants in stock**. At $40 it's a fraction of the Jamroc picks
above while still publishing a real fabric spec — not just "soft, comfortable" marketing copy.

### ❤️ Would appreciate a cause behind it — Better Together Hooded Sweatshirt (Black), Stay Another Day ($54.95)

A **plain, solid-color hoodie with no loud graphic** — just the "Better Together" name tied to the
brand's mental-health mission (the brand prints the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline number on its
core line; this specific piece is the low-key version of that). Sizes **Small through 5XL**, **8 of
8 variants in stock**. The pick for a dad who'd rather not wear a big logo but still likes what the
purchase supports.

## Who should think twice before buying a hoodie as the gift

- ❌ **He lives somewhere that doesn't get cold.** A hoodie sitting unused in a drawer isn't a great
  gift regardless of quality — check the [Father's Day gift-category data](#sources) below for
  alternatives if climate doesn't fit.
- ❌ **You're not sure of his size and can't ask.** Every pick above has a real size ceiling (the
  Resilience hoodie specifically stops at XL) — guessing wrong on a gift is worse than not giving one.
- ❌ **He specifically wants performance/technical wear** (moisture-wicking, running gear). None of
  these four brands are athletic-performance brands — they're streetwear and casualwear.
