Surf Trip Supply is a small California-flavored surf and streetwear label: hand-drawn graphics,
100% cotton, and hoodies that mostly land between **$80 and $98**. For an indie brand a lot of
people haven't heard of, that's a real ask — so the fair question isn't "is it cool," it's
**"is it worth it?"**

We broke down exactly what that money buys, using Surf Trip Supply's own live product data. The
honest answer: for a specific kind of buyer, it's genuinely a good pick — and for another kind,
it's the wrong shop. Let's make sure you know which one you are first.

## What your money buys

Surf Trip Supply's catalog is small and focused — **31 apparel styles**, priced **$55 to $135**,
with a median around **$84**. The core of it is **14 hoodies at $80–98**, plus tees ($55), knit
sweaters ($84), button-up shirts ($65–75), sweatpants and matching sweat sets ($75 / $130), a
crewneck ($80), board trunks ($75), denim ($76) and a reversible jacket ($135).

Two things stand out in that data, and both are unusual for a brand this size.

**1. It tells you how it fits.** On **28 of 31 styles**, the listing names the model's height and
the exact size they're wearing — "Kai is 6'3 and wears a large," "model is 5'6 wearing a medium for
an oversized fit." Most brands make you guess. This is the single most useful thing a small label
can publish, and Surf Trip Supply does it almost everywhere.

**2. It's mostly honest, heavy-ish cotton.** **15 of 31 styles explicitly state 100% cotton**, with
a relaxed or boxy cut and clear "size up for oversized" guidance. The designs are original — the
Shark Embroidered Hoodie is hand-drawn by founder Larry and finished with what the brand describes
as over nine hours of embroidery. That's a made-thing, not a generic blank with a stock print.

## The one number to know: the Toggle Hoodie

Here's the catch for value shoppers: **only 4 of 31 styles publish a fabric weight (GSM)** — the
one metric that lets you compare hoodies honestly. That's the Toggle Hoodie at **460 GSM ($98)** and
three jacquard knit sweaters at **260 GSM ($84)**. Everything else says "100% cotton" but not how
heavy.

Where they *do* publish it, the value is genuinely good. Put the Toggle Hoodie in cost-per-gram terms:

— a genuinely heavy, thermal-lined-hood hoodie for under $100. The honest limitation: it's the *only*
Surf Trip hoodie with a published weight, so you can't assume the same density across the rest of the
line. If fabric weight is how you shop, buy the Toggle — and treat the unlabeled styles as unknowns.

## Where the price is fair — and where it isn't

**Fair:** the **fit transparency and original design.** You're buying a hand-drawn surf aesthetic you
won't see everywhere, from a brand that actually tells you how each piece fits. For a lot of buyers,
that's exactly the premium worth paying at this price.

**Fair:** the **Toggle Hoodie** specifically — 460 GSM for $98 is real heavyweight cotton at a fair
cost per gram.

**Less fair:** **sizing.** The range essentially caps at **XL** (a single knit sweater reaches XXL),
with no 2XL-and-up options. If you wear larger sizes, this brand simply isn't built for you — and
that's a hard limit, not a preference.

**Depends:** **availability.** These are small batches — listings say "limited edition," "very
limited quantities," even "under 100 made." Right now **6 of 31 styles are sold out in every size.**
The scarcity is part of the appeal for some buyers and a dealbreaker for others.
