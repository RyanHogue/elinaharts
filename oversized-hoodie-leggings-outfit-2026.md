## How to style the rest of the outfit (leggings + sneakers)

We don't have an affiliate program for leggings, so nothing below links to a specific product —
this is general guidance pulled from the same trend coverage that named this outfit combination,
not a shopping list.

- **Color:** Trend write-ups consistently pair a colorful or pastel hoodie with **black or charcoal
  leggings**, not a second bright color — the hoodie is the statement piece, the leggings are the
  neutral base.
- **Rise and coverage:** Look for **high-waisted leggings with opacity you'd trust for a squat-test
  in-store** — this is standard advice across the trend pieces we read, not brand-specific.
- **Sneakers:** **Thick white or cream chunky sneakers, worn with visible socks**, are the footwear
  consistently named alongside this outfit — not slides or slim running shoes.
- **Proportion:** For an oversized hoodie specifically, trend guidance suggests it should hit
  roughly **mid-thigh** — noticeably past the hip, but not so long it hides the leggings entirely.
