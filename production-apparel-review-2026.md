## The niche is real, and intentional

Almost every product name references film/production terminology:

- *Stranger Call Times* — references the "call time" printed on production call sheets
- *Aspect Ratios* — the cinematographer's constant math (16:9, 4:3, 2.39:1)
- *Camera Hands* — nickname for the camera assistant on set
- *2nd AC Abbrevs* — shorthand for "Second Assistant Camera" (the focus puller's role)
- *International Sound Grip Guild* — a riff on International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW)

This is **not** generic streetwear pretending to be street. Production Apparel is deliberately built for people who live in production — the joke lands because you recognize the reference.

That makes it a **tight niche**. If you're a filmmaker, a DP's assistant, or someone deep in indie film or commercial production, you get it immediately. If you're not, the brand reads as obscure.

**That's not a flaw** — it's a feature. Niche is honest. You know exactly who this is for.

Visual inspection of the site would confirm, but the data structure suggests Production Apparel may cap sizing at **XL**, not 2XL or higher. This is a hard limit for buyers who wear larger sizes.
