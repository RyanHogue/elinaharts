Stay Another Day's catalog runs close to 130 apparel styles, and a lot of them look like near
duplicates at first glance — same message, same silhouette, two different prices. That's not a
mistake in their store: nearly every core design comes in **two versions, screen printed and
embroidered**, and the difference isn't fabric quality. We pulled the brand's own product data for
both versions to show you exactly what changes.

## What screen printing gets you

The screen-printed version puts more design on the garment: a small front logo plus text, and a
larger graphic on the back. It's the cheaper option across every garment type we checked — $28 for
a tee, $50 for a crewneck, $55 for a hoodie — and it's also the format with the most design variety
in the catalog. Of the roughly 130 styles in Stay Another Day's line, the large majority are
screen-printed designs; embroidery is offered on a smaller, curated subset (around 18 SKUs across
five core designs).

## What embroidery gets you

The embroidered version is a single design, centered on the front chest, with no back print. It
costs consistently more — $35 for a tee, $58 for a crewneck, $63 for a hoodie — for what the
brand's own listings describe as the same design "embroidered" rather than printed. Embroidery is
also available on a long-sleeve tee ($43) in some designs, a format not offered in screen print for
every design in the line.

If the appeal for you is the look of embroidery specifically — a raised, stitched logo instead of a
flat print — that's what you're paying the $7–8 premium for. It's a legitimate reason to pick it;
just know it's not a heavier hoodie or a different cotton blend underneath.

## Who should NOT pay extra for embroidery

- ❌ **You want the back-print design.** Embroidery is front-chest only in every listing we checked
  — if the larger graphic on the back is part of why you like a design, screen print is the only
  way to get it.
- ❌ **You're choosing based on expected durability.** We have no published data — from the brand or
  independent sources — comparing how the two hold up over time. Don't pay more assuming embroidery
  outlasts print; we can't confirm that either way.
- ❌ **You want the widest design selection.** Embroidery is offered on roughly five core designs;
  screen printing covers the rest of the catalog.
- ❌ **You're buying for a child.** Youth sizing (the two we found: "You Are Loved" embroidered youth
  hoodie and crewneck) is priced closer to $28–29, similar to adult screen-print pricing — check the
  specific listing rather than assuming the adult price gap applies.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is embroidered Stay Another Day merchandise better quality than screen printed?**
Not in terms of the base garment — both use the same blank (Gildan Heavyweight Fleece for hoodies/
crewnecks, Comfort Colors ring-spun cotton for tees). The difference is the decoration technique and
design layout, not the fabric.

**Why is embroidery more expensive if the shirt is the same?**
Embroidery is generally a more labor- and machine-intensive process than screen printing, which is
reflected in the consistent $7–8 premium across every garment type we checked. That's a normal
industry pricing pattern, not specific to this brand.

**Does the embroidered version still have 988 on it?**
Our [full brand review]({{ '/stay-another-day-review-2026' | relative_url }}) covers the 988
Suicide & Crisis Lifeline detail across the line generally; check the specific product photos for
placement, since embroidery's front-chest-only format changes where design elements can appear
compared to a screen print with a back graphic.

**Which one should I buy if I can't decide?**
If you want the design most people would recognize (the larger back graphic), buy screen printed.
If you specifically want a more subtle, single front-chest logo — the kind of thing that reads as a
plain hoodie until someone's close enough to see it — the embroidered version is built for that, and
that's worth the $7–8 to you or it isn't.

**Does Stay Another Day say how much of each sale goes to charity?**
No — the brand states that "a portion of every sale will be donated to the 988 Suicide & Crisis
Lifeline," but does not publish a specific percentage or dollar figure, for either the screen-printed
or embroidered lines. See our [full review]({{ '/stay-another-day-review-2026' | relative_url }})
for more on this.
