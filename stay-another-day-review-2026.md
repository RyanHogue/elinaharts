Stay Another Day is a **mental-health awareness apparel brand** out of Bloomington, Illinois. The
whole idea is in the name: the clothing carries messages meant to spark conversation about mental
health, and the number **988** — the US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — is printed on every item, often
tucked somewhere small.

If you found this brand and you're wondering *is it legit, what do you actually get, and is it worth
buying* — that's the right set of questions for a cause brand, and this breakdown answers all three
using the brand's own product data and stated commitments.

## What the brand is really about

Stay Another Day describes itself as a mental-health awareness brand built to break the stigma
around asking for help — its own words: many people "suffer in silence because they are either
afraid to ask, or don't know how to get help." The clothing is the conversation starter, and the
988 number is the practical hook baked into every piece.

On giving back, the brand states on its site that **"a portion of every sale will be donated to the
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline,"** and that it also allocates part of each sale to giving shirts to
kids through schools. That is the brand's own stated commitment — **no specific percentage or dollar
figure is published**, and we haven't independently audited it. If the exact split matters to you,
it's a fair question to ask the brand directly before buying.

The takeaway: with this brand, part of what you're paying for is the cause and the message, not just
the shirt. That's the honest frame for the rest of this review.

## What you actually get

The catalog is **around 120 apparel styles** — roughly **37 t-shirts, 30 hoodies, 23 crewnecks and
15 long-sleeve tees**, plus kids' pieces, onesies and drinkware. Prices run **$6 to $75**, with a
**median around $35** and tees frequently under $30. (A couple of $150–$600 listings are "giveaway"
shirt packages and gift cards, not everyday products.)

**The blanks are named, and that's useful.** Stay Another Day doesn't publish fabric weights, but it
does tell you what it prints on — mainly **Comfort Colors** tees and **Gildan Heavy Blend** hoodies.
Those are well-documented industry blanks, so you can know what you're getting:

| Piece | Blank | Documented spec |
|---|---|---|
| Tees | Comfort Colors (e.g. 1717) | **6.1 oz, 100% ring-spun cotton**, garment-dyed (~207 GSM) |
| Hoodies | Gildan Heavy Blend (18500) | **8 oz, 50% cotton / 50% polyester** fleece |

A Comfort Colors tee is a genuinely nicer blank than a basic promotional shirt — heavier, softer,
and pigment-dyed for that lived-in vintage color. The Gildan Heavy Blend hoodie is a solid, familiar
mid-weight — not a heavyweight streetwear piece, but a dependable everyday hoodie. Note the brand
doesn't state exact style numbers, so treat these as the standard specs for those lines.

**Color and size range are real strengths.** The tees come in a large Comfort Colors palette (black,
white, light blue, pink, army green, mauve, coral and many more), and **most styles offer up to 2XL
or 3XL** — better extended-size coverage than a lot of small brands.

## Where it's a good buy — and where to keep expectations in check

**Good buy:** if you want to **wear the message, support the cause, and get a decent Comfort Colors
tee or Gildan hoodie** in a color and size you like, this is a fair, affordable way to do it. The
message designs are original, the pricing is reasonable, and the cause is front-and-center.

**Keep expectations in check:** this is **print-on-quality-blanks**, not custom-milled heavyweight
fabric. If you're shopping the way you'd shop a specs-first streetwear brand — comparing GSM, fibre
density and cut — this brand isn't built for that, and it doesn't publish those numbers. You're
paying a modest premium over a plain blank, and that premium is the design and the giving, not
exotic construction.

**One honest note on value:** a blank Comfort Colors tee wholesale is inexpensive, so part of your
price is the cause and the artwork. For this kind of brand that's the point — just buy it for the
right reason.
