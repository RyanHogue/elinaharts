## Short answer

**No. Stay Another Day makes no "runs small" claim anywhere in its catalog.** We read the product
data for **97 adult apparel styles**, and zero use "runs small," "runs large," "true to size," or
any sizing warning at all.

But there's a real, non-obvious pattern hiding in the data — and it's more useful than a fit
warning would be: **which size range you can actually get depends on what kind of garment you buy,
not on the design.**

⚠️ **Buy a t-shirt or long sleeve, and your options mostly stop at 3XL.** Buy a hoodie or
crewneck, and **the same design is very likely available up to 5XL.** This isn't random — it comes
straight from which blank garment the brand prints on for each category.

## The pattern: your size range depends on the garment, not the design

Stay Another Day repeats most of its message designs — "988," "Be Kind To Your Mind," "You Are
Loved" — across four garment types: t-shirt, long sleeve, crewneck, hoodie. It's tempting to assume
a design's sizing is consistent across those four versions. **It isn't**, because each garment type
is printed on a different blank with its own size run:

| Garment type | Styles checked | Named blank | Sizes reaching 4XL+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hoodie** | 25 | Gildan Heavyweight Fleece (50/50 cotton-poly) | **21 of 25 (84%)** reach 5XL |
| **Crewneck** | 20 | Gildan Heavyweight Fleece (50/50 cotton-poly) | **17 of 20 (85%)** reach 5XL |
| **T-Shirt** | 31 | Comfort Colors (100% ring-spun cotton) | **5 of 31 (16%)** reach 4XL or 5XL |
| **Long Sleeve** | 16 | Comfort Colors (100% ring-spun cotton) | **1 of 16 (6%)** reaches 4XL; none reach 5XL |

Take the **988 design** as the clearest example, since it's sold as all four types: the **988
Screen Printed Hoodie** and **988 Screen Printed Crewneck** are both listed "Available from Small
thru 5x." The **988 Screen Printed T-shirt** is listed "Available from Small thru 4x." The **988
Screen Printed Long Sleeve** stops at **3x**. Same artwork, same brand, three different size
ceilings — because each is a different blank.

**What this means practically:** if you or the person you're buying for wears above 3XL, look at
the hoodie and crewneck versions of a design first. The t-shirt and long sleeve versions of that
exact same design may simply not exist in that size.

## The two blanks, and what each one tells you about fit

Stay Another Day doesn't publish its own fabric weights or measurements, but it names the blanks it
prints on — and those are documented, well-known garments in the printing industry, so their specs
are checkable independent of the brand.

**Comfort Colors (t-shirts and long sleeves).** The brand's own listing language calls these
**"Relaxed Fit"** — stated word-for-word on the majority of tee and long-sleeve pages (30 of the 47
we checked). Comfort Colors is a garment-dyed, 100% ring-spun cotton line, which behaves differently
from a plain printed tee: it's pre-shrunk during dyeing and has a slightly looser, broken-in feel
out of the bag, not a tight retail-fresh cut. Order your usual size for the relaxed fit the brand
describes; there's no "size up" instruction attached to it.

**Gildan Heavyweight Fleece (hoodies and crewnecks).** These listings carry **no fit adjective at
all** — no "relaxed," no "oversized," nothing. Gildan Heavy Blend fleece is a standard mid-weight
50/50 cotton-polyester construction, the same blank used across a huge share of the printed-apparel
industry — familiar, true-to-size-leaning, not a boxy streetwear cut. If you've worn a mainstream
Gildan hoodie before, you already know roughly how this one will sit.

Neither blank is unusual or risky to order in your normal size. The thing to plan around isn't fit
— it's which one actually stocks the size you need.

## Unisex sizing, and what that means here

**75 of the 97 adult styles are explicitly labeled unisex.** Stay Another Day doesn't publish
separate women's-cut sizing at all — every hoodie, crewneck, tee and long sleeve in this catalog is
built on the same straight, unisex block. If you're buying for someone who normally wears a
women's-specific fitted cut, expect this to sit roomier than that, in line with the "Relaxed Fit"
language on the tees and the standard Gildan block on the fleece pieces. There's no fitted or
cropped alternative here to size around instead.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Stay Another Day run small?**
No. Nothing in the catalog carries a "runs small" warning, and the tees are explicitly described as
"Relaxed Fit." Order your usual size.

**What's the largest size Stay Another Day offers?**
5XL — but only reliably on hoodies and crewnecks (Gildan Heavyweight Fleece). T-shirts and long
sleeves (Comfort Colors) mostly stop at 3XL, with only a handful reaching 4XL.

**Why does the same design come in different size ranges?**
Because each garment type — t-shirt, long sleeve, crewneck, hoodie — is printed on a different blank
with its own size run. The design doesn't change the range; the garment type does.

**Are the shirts true to size or relaxed?**
The brand's own language calls the Comfort Colors tees and long sleeves "Relaxed Fit." The Gildan
hoodies and crewnecks carry no fit adjective, which in this industry generally reads as a standard,
true-to-size block.

**Does Stay Another Day offer women's sizing?**
No — 75 of 97 adult styles are explicitly unisex, and there's no separate women's-cut line. Expect a
straight, roomier fit than a fitted women's tee.

**Do colors run out at larger sizes?**
Not in what we checked — the full color range we sampled was available across the entire size run,
small through the largest size offered, including 5XL hoodies.

**How does this compare to the brand's other specs?**
