## What Suadex actually is

Suadex sells **steel-toe safety shoes and work boots for adults** — sneaker-style and boot-style footwear aimed at construction, warehouse, and industrial workers who need OSHA-relevant toe protection. It is not a children's shoe brand, and it has no back-to-school angle.

- **23 products** in the current catalog, all adult footwear
- **Price range:** $39.99–$56.99
- **Styles:** roughly split between "sneaker" (lightweight, steel-toe) and "boots" (taller, heavier-duty)
- **Marketed as:** unisex ("Men Women") on most listings
