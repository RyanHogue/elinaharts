## The method: match your height to the model's

Most sizing guides work from a chest or length measurement. Surf Trip Supply's catalog barely has
any of those — so its real sizing tool is the height-and-size line built into most listings. Reading
across all 26 styles that include one, a clean pattern shows up:

| Model height | Size they wear | Example products |
|---|---|---|
| 5'4"–5'6" | Medium | 90s Hoodie, Sea Shell Zipup Hoodie, Brown Hoodie, Electric Blue Hoodie, Dolphin Knit Sweater, Reversible Jacket |
| 5'8" | Medium | Electric Blue Hoodie, Y2K Female Denim |
| 5'10" | Large | Ripper Hoodie Brown, Y2K Red Hoodie, Shark Knit Sweater (Navy & Brown) |
| 6'2" | Large | Oahu Trunks |
| 6'2" | **Medium** ⚠️ | **Toggle Hoodie** — the one exception |
| 6'3" | Large | Ripper Tee Brown, Y2K Navy Hoodie |

Two models sit right at 5'4" and land on different sizes for a reason worth flagging: on the Shark
Hoodie, a 5'4" model wears a Large, but the listing says that's *"for an oversized fit"* — a
deliberate size-up, not the standard recommendation. Read the fit phrase next to the name, not just
the size.

**Practical use:** if a model close to your height is wearing a Medium across several styles, order
Medium as your default in this catalog — then check the individual listing for a fit note before
you commit, because a few styles (see below) add "size up" or run their own way.

## Fit vocabulary, decoded

These words repeat across the catalog, and each one changes what the height table above actually
means for you:

| Term | Appears on | What it means for sizing |
|---|---|---|
| **Oversized** | 15 of 33 styles | Deliberately roomy. Order the size the height table suggests — don't compensate further, except on the Toggle Hoodie. |
| **Relaxed** | 11 | A gentler version of oversized. Order normally. |
| **Boxy** | 10 | Wide, square silhouette, usually on hoodies and crewnecks. Order normally; this is the intended shape. |
| **True to size / true-to-size** | 11 | No adjustment needed — the sweatpants and a few sweatshirts use this label specifically. |
| **Unisex** | 5 | Cut on a straight block. The two button-ups explicitly say a shorter model (5'4") takes a Small and a taller one (6'2") takes a Large — so still use the height match. |
| **Loose** | 5 | Same guidance as relaxed/boxy — order your normal size. |
| **Cropped** | 2 | Shorter body length only — the two boxy button-up shirts. Doesn't affect width sizing. |

Notice there's no "fitted" or "slim" label anywhere in this catalog — Surf Trip Supply doesn't sell
a close-cut silhouette. Every style leans relaxed-to-oversized, which is also why the height-match
method works as well as it does: there's less variance in shape to account for.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Surf Trip Supply run small?**
No. Nothing in the catalog is described as running small, and none of the 33 styles carry that
warning. If anything, the flagship Toggle Hoodie runs large.

**How do I pick a size without a measurement chart?**
Match your height to the closest model in the product description — most listings state the
model's height and the size they're wearing. Under 5'9", that's usually a Medium; 5'9" and up,
usually a Large. Four knitwear styles also have an image-based size chart on the product page.

**Does the Toggle Hoodie run large?**
Yes — the brand's own listing says "Fits oversized (size down)," and a 6'2" model wears a Medium in
it, versus a Large in every other hoodie at that height. Size down from what the general height
table suggests.

**Does Surf Trip Supply have an actual measurement chart?**
On four styles (Noah Crewneck and three Jacquard knit sweaters), yes — but it's posted as an image
on the product page, not published as text we could extract and quote. Check that image directly for
those four; use the height-match method for everything else.

**Do they offer plus sizes?**
Barely. One style — a Jacquard knit sweater — reaches XXL, and as of this writing it's sold out in
that size. Every other style stops at XL.

**Is Surf Trip Supply true to size or oversized?**
Neither term applies to the whole catalog uniformly — 15 of 33 styles are explicitly described as
"oversized" and 11 as "relaxed," with 11 more labeled "true to size" (mostly sweatpants). Read the
fit word on the specific product; there's no single answer for the brand as a whole.

**Why does this guide use height instead of a chest measurement?**
Because that's what the brand itself publishes. Unlike brands that print inch-by-inch charts, Surf
Trip Supply's product pages tell you a model's height and the size they wear on 26 of 33 styles —
so that comparison is the most reliable sizing signal actually available.
