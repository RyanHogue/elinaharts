## The capsule

### 🩳 Swim trunks — Oahu Trunks ($75)

The brand's own fit note: a 6'2" model wears a Large "for the perfect fit," and the listing describes
the fit as true to size — a useful anchor if you know the wearer's height (see our
[sizing guide]({{ '/surf-trip-supply-sizing-guide-2026' | relative_url }}) for the brand's general
height-to-size pattern). Sizes S–XL, **3 of 4 sizes in stock** at the time we checked — one size is
currently sold out, so confirm yours before ordering.

### 🩴 Sandals — Flip Flops ($75)

The brand's simplest listing, described only as "true to size." Sized by shoe number (Mens 5/Womens
6.5 through Mens 13/Womens 14.5), **3 of 9 sizes in stock** — this one has the tightest availability
of anything in this capsule, so check your exact size before counting on it.

### 👖 Linen Pants ($64.80)

Described by the brand as "unisex, baggy-fit... lightweight and breathable... perfect for everything
from beach strolls to laid-back hangouts," with an elastic waistband. This is the one piece here
that isn't swimwear or a tee — the layer that takes you from the beach to dinner without changing
completely. Sizes S–XL, **2 of 4 in stock**.

### 👔 Layering shirt — Blue Button Up ($65)

A cropped, boxy button-up with frayed seams — the brand's own copy calls the look "laid-back and
pulled-together" with "surf-camp edge." Unisex sizing, **4 of 4 in stock**, the best-stocked piece in
this whole capsule. Works as a light cover-up over swimwear or on its own in the evening.

### 👕 Everyday tee — Ripper Tee, Brown ($55)

100% cotton, with the brand's fit note specific enough to plan around: "Kai is 6'3 and wears a
large... size up if in between sizes." This is the piece you actually wear most days of the trip —
**only 2 of 4 sizes are in stock right now** (Medium and Large), so if you need Small or XL, this
isn't the pick today.

### 🧥 For cool evenings — Shark Knit Sweater, Navy ($84)

Beach trips still get cool after sunset, and this is the one piece in the capsule with a published
fabric weight: **260 GSM**, a 40% wool / 40% polyester / 20% acrylic jacquard knit, described as a
"loose, boxy fit." It's a genuinely different material from the brand's cotton hoodies — closer to a
sweater — and **4 of 4 sizes are in stock**.
