All three Y2K hoodies are $89 and 100% cotton, but the brand gives a different fit anchor for each

**Y2K Red Hoodie** — the listing anchors to a 5'10" model wearing a Large, which lines up with the
brand's general pattern (our sizing guide found models under 5'9" typically wear a Medium, 5'9" and
up typically wear a Large — this one is right at that boundary, sized to the taller end).

**Y2K Navy Hoodie** — anchored to a 6'3" model, also in a Large. That's notable: at 6'3", a Large
runs looser/more oversized on this person than it would on someone at the brand's usual 5'9"–6'0"
range for that size. If you're above 6'2", this listing is useful evidence the Large still works,
not just the Medium/Large boundary most other styles reference.

**Y2K Pink Hoodie** — the only one of the three with no model reference at all; the listing just
says "true to size." That's a real gap for this specific style — we can't tell you a height/size
pairing because the brand didn't publish one, so if you're between sizes, the safer move is to size
