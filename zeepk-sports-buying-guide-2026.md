## What Zeepk Sports actually is

Zeepk Sports sells **sporting equipment**, not shoes and not clothing — the brand's real catalog is dominated by **cricket bats** and combat-sport gear.

- **23 products**, price range **$9.99–$49.99**
- **16 of 23 products are cricket bats**, most made from Kashmir Willow, priced $39.99–$49.99
- The remainder: boxing/MMA gloves and headgear, shin guards, a soccer ball, soccer goalie gloves, and a couple of kids' cricket/baseball combo sets

| Product type | Examples | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Cricket bats (adult) | "5 Star Series," "Katana," "Sparta" — Kashmir Willow, tennis-ball/tape-ball style | $39.99–$49.99 |
| Cricket bats/sets (kids) | "Cricket Kit for Kids," ages 8–12, includes bat + wickets | $39.99 |
| Combat sport gear | MMA/boxing gloves, headgear, shin guards | $9.99–$49.99 |
| Ball sports | Soccer ball, goalie gloves, cricket balls | $14.99–$29.99 |
| Kids | Plastic baseball bat & ball combo | $9.99 |

There is **no footwear and no apparel** anywhere in the catalog we pulled — no shoes, no shirts, no hoodies.

---

## Who this is actually for

✅ **Cricket players or parents buying a kids' starter cricket set** — this is genuinely the brand's core product
✅ **Casual boxing/MMA training** — mid-range gloves and headgear at accessible prices

❌ **Anyone looking for kids' school shoes or athletic apparel** — this brand doesn't sell either
❌ **Serious/competitive athletes** — this reads as recreational-grade equipment at recreational prices, not performance gear with published technical specs
